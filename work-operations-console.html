02/Operations software

An operations console for a distributed team

Four tools, a shared inbox and a spreadsheet replaced by one interface, with roles, audit trails and reporting designed in from the first week rather than added under pressure.

Illustrative case study. This describes how Cloudocol runs an engagement of this shape. It is not an account of a specific client project, and no client is named or implied. Published case studies will replace these pages as they are cleared for release.

01/The problem

The work got done, but only because a handful of people held the process in their heads. Status lived in an inbox, exceptions lived in a spreadsheet, and reporting meant someone reconciling both on a Friday.

Onboarding a new coordinator took weeks. Nobody could answer how long a job actually took, because no system recorded the moment it changed hands.

02/Approach

Software cannot fix a process nobody has agreed on. First write it down.

Two weeks of sitting with the team before any design work. We mapped the real process, including the informal steps, and found three places where two people were doing the same job.

The build followed the state machine rather than the screens: every job has a defined status, every transition is recorded with who and when, and the interface is a view onto that. Reporting stopped being a feature and became a by-product.

03/What shipped

One console, four capabilities.

A single queue
Every live job in one view, filtered by role, with the next action obvious without opening the record.
Roles and permissions
Coordinators, field staff and finance each see what they need and cannot alter what they should not.
An audit trail
Every status change recorded with actor, timestamp and prior value. Exportable, and used to answer disputes.
Reporting by default
Cycle time, throughput and exception rate generated from the same data the console runs on, so no reconciliation step exists.
04/What we measure

Success, defined before the build.

Numbers here are the ones we agree at the start and instrument on day one — not figures assembled afterwards to make a case study read well.

Primary
Median time from job created to closed
Secondary
Jobs handled per coordinator per week
Operational
Time for a new coordinator to work unsupervised
Guardrail
Exception and rework rate, tracked against the old baseline
