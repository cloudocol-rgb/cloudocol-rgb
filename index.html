01/Services

From idea to digital reality.

Three practices that work as one team. Most engagements draw on all of them, which is why we keep them in the same room rather than the same org chart.

Practice 01

Design

Interfaces people understand on first use, and a visual system that still holds together two years later.

  • 01 UI/UX design Interface and interaction design, from first wireframe to production-ready screens.
  • 02 Product design Shaping what gets built and in what order, tied to the outcome you're measured on.
  • 03 Design systems Tokens, components and documentation your team can build on without us.
  • 04 Branding & visual identity Identity work made for screens first — logo, type, colour and the rules that hold them.
  • 05 UX research Interviews, usability testing and behavioural data that settle arguments with evidence.
Practice 02

Development

Software written to be read, extended and handed over. Performance and accessibility are part of the build, not a later pass.

  • 06 Web development Marketing sites, portals and web apps built on React, Next.js and TypeScript.
  • 07 Mobile app development iOS and Android, native or cross-platform, released through your own store accounts.
  • 08 Custom software Internal tools and line-of-business systems that replace the spreadsheet everyone dreads.
  • 09 API & backend development Services, data models and integrations designed for the load you'll actually see.
  • 10 E-commerce Storefronts, checkout and payments — measured on conversion, not page count.
Practice 03

Technology

The layer underneath: infrastructure, automation and the judgement calls about what to build, buy or retire.

  • 11 Cloud solutions Architecture, migration and cost control across AWS, Azure and Google Cloud.
  • 12 AI & automation Applied where it removes real work — document handling, support, internal search.
  • 13 DevOps Pipelines, environments and monitoring so releases stop being an event.
  • 14 System integration Connecting the tools you already run, including the ones nobody wants to touch.
  • 15 IT consulting Technical due diligence, platform choices and roadmaps, with the trade-offs written down.
02/How we work

Think. Design. Build. Evolve.

Four stages, one continuous engagement. You see working software long before the last one.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We start with the business and the people using it — what the opportunity is, what's in the way, and what success looks like in numbers.

    Stakeholder interviewsTechnical auditScope & estimate
  2. 02

    Design

    Ideas become something you can click. We test the risky parts early, while changing them is still cheap.

    Flows & wireframesPrototypesDesign system
  3. 03

    Build

    Engineering in short cycles with a working build at the end of each one. Infrastructure, testing and documentation ship alongside the features.

    Two-week cyclesCI/CDQA & accessibility
  4. 04

    Evolve

    After launch we watch how it behaves in the real world and keep improving it — performance, cost, and the next set of features.

    AnalyticsOptimisationOngoing support

04/Why Cloudocol

Technology is only valuable when it solves the right problem.

Design-led thinking

Design decides how the work is understood. It shapes the brief rather than dressing the result.

Business-first

Every feature is tied to a commercial reason. If we can't find one, we say so before you pay for it.

Engineering you can inherit

Clear architecture, tests and documentation. Your next developer should not need an archaeologist.

Built to scale sensibly

Systems sized for the next stage of growth, not a hypothetical one that never arrives.

Human-centred

We test with the people who'll use it, including the ones who never asked for new software.

Long-term partnership

Most of our work is with clients we've already shipped for. That shapes how we scope the first project.
