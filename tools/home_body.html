02/How we work

Think. Design. Build. Evolve.

Four stages, one continuous engagement. You see working software long before the last one.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We start with the business and the people using it — what the opportunity is, what's in the way, and what success looks like in numbers.

    Stakeholder interviewsTechnical auditScope & estimate
  2. 02

    Design

    Ideas become something you can click. We test the risky parts early, while changing them is still cheap.

    Flows & wireframesPrototypesDesign system
  3. 03

    Build

    Engineering in short cycles with a working build at the end of each one. Infrastructure, testing and documentation ship alongside the features.

    Two-week cyclesCI/CDQA & accessibility
  4. 04

    Evolve

    After launch we watch how it behaves in the real world and keep improving it — performance, cost, and the next set of features.

    AnalyticsOptimisationOngoing support

Design-led thinking

Design decides how the work is understood. It shapes the brief rather than dressing the result.

Business-first

Engineering you can inherit

Clear architecture, tests and documentation. Your next developer should not need an archaeologist.

Built to scale sensibly

Systems sized for the next stage of growth, not a hypothetical one that never arrives.

Human-centred

Long-term partnership
